Create an original, polished Korean automotive coaching app UI concept for novice drivers. The output is the flat FRONT-ON app content itself, not a photograph of a tablet, not a perspective device mockup. Very wide canvas, target 2560 x 1268 pixels, approximately 2.02:1 aspect ratio, fill the canvas edge to edge. No car OS bars or browser chrome.
Art direction: TOEM-inspired quiet black-and-white hand-inked simplicity combined with Paper Mario-inspired physical cut-paper construction, translated to an original product interface. Thick, confident but gently irregular charcoal outlines; restrained whimsical drawn vehicles; real matte off-white paper fibers; thin cut-paper edge highlights; visibly separate overlapping sheets; short, soft lower-right contact shadows from consistent upper-left lighting. Layer depth should be clearly visible but subtle and functional. Avoid generic slick SaaS panels, glossy plastic, clay, bevelled 3D buttons, heavy distressed/grunge paper, watercolor washes, excessive tape/confetti, noisy hatch marks behind text.
Palette: warm ivory background #F2EDDF, clean cream card surfaces #FCF8EF, charcoal ink #30332F, soft gray secondary ink, muted sage #B7C99B for selected/positive state, muted ochre #C29B58 for simulation/caution. Most of the image is neutral. Clean high-contrast Korean sans-serif text, not handwritten body text. Large titles and comfortably readable labels; precise Hangul spelling. Hand-drawn character belongs in the edges and illustrations, not distorted letters. No arbitrary new copy. No TOEM or Nintendo logos/characters. No screenshots of Steam or YouTube.
Use supplied references by role: the TOEM screenshot informs line art, simplicity, tone only; the Paper Mario screenshot informs layered paper materials, edge thickness and contact shadows only; any app screenshot informs actual content and state, never its green dark theme. If a finished paper UI reference is supplied, inherit its material, line weight, palette, corner treatment and text hierarchy exactly.
